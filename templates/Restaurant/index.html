chef aravind

CHEF Aravind Nair

Aravind’s culinary creations are reflective of his own extensive travels and love of combining spices from all over the Indian subcontinent. His cooking focuses on slow-cooked tandoori dishes sourced from organic local farmers and he pays thoughtful attention to ingredients and subtle saffron notes.

Food Philosophy

Our daily changing menu represents the best of what our local markets have to offer. Our passion for sourcing fresh ingredients from farmers and suppliers we know, combining with traditional flavors and rewriting familiar recipes into exceptional cuisine is our mission and greatest joy.
